Warranty Claim & Service Lifecycle

7-step end-to-end flow across Consumer, Dealer, and Manufacturer.

1
Consumer Reports Problem
Product linked automatically via Digital Twin. No need to re-enter serial, invoice, or purchase info.
Consumer
2
Dealer Inspects & Diagnoses
Serial / warranty status verified against the platform.
Dealer
3
Dealer Creates Claim
Diagnosis + evidence submitted. Claim inherits product, customer, dealer, and purchase details automatically.
Dealer
4
Manufacturer Reviews Claim
System checks warranty status using purchase date, warranty rules, and product identity.
Manufacturer
5
Repair / Replace
Parts and labour recorded. Dealer receives product for repair/replacement if applicable.
Dealer
6
Consumer Receives Resolution
Consumer confirms resolution. Claim status visible throughout.
Consumer
7
Reimbursement & Closure
Manufacturer reimburses dealer. Approved claim closed; service history attached to Digital Twin.
Manufacturer
Automatic decision: If warranty expired, consumer is shown warranty status and available next steps.
